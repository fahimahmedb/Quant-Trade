# Advanced Research decision: ONLY_AS_A_THIN_POLICY_LAYER

A distinct new Advanced Research subsystem is **NOT_YET justified**. If that name is used, its only defensible present meaning is a thin research-program policy inside existing Research OS/Research Intelligence, not another agent, runtime, admission ledger, Discovery Engine, Factory or adjudicator.

## Prove the function before naming it

The missing function is: **choose and record a lawful successor to a finite research program, using source availability, negative knowledge and charged exposure; ensure each permitted positive handoff receives one durable next disposition.** The first half lacks an explicit renewal transaction; the second half has existing owners but incomplete transitions. Neither requires a new top-level system.

Existing pieces can perform the work:

- Research Intelligence evaluates qualified evidence/memory, next actions and search completeness without owning runtime mutation.
- Research OS/Control Plane owns budgets, task creation, claims, scheduling, restart and receipts. Its source-aware replenisher already avoids consumed packets on the W2 path.
- F supplies source federation, seed/root/exposure and bounded feed primitives where needed. Its fixture-origin breadth is not proof of live discovery quality.
- V3 is a candidate admission contract with durable rejection/replay; it ends at eligibility for existing Discovery. Choose one admission contract instead of retaining competing proposal truths.
- Discovery creates Candidates; Factory freezes experiments; C0 adjudicates claim scope; Economic/Portfolio/D0 and Desk/Book/Learning retain their existing responsibilities.

See [ownership matrix](03_CAPABILITY_OVERLAP_MATRIX.csv), [renewal](04_FRONTIER_RENEWAL_ANALYSIS.md), [continuation](05_POSITIVE_CONTINUATION_ANALYSIS.md), and [quality/denominators](14_SCIENTIFIC_QUALITY_AND_DENOMINATORS.md). No component gains authority merely by being upstream of a scientific pass.

First compare the zero-new-architecture Option Z: targeted recovery and lawful authority/source/task configuration may solve individual lineages. Add a thin contract only where that baseline cannot express the preserved-history successor or required next disposition. The confirmed W2 metadata/counting mismatches need existing source repairs rather than a new research owner.

## Unique policy contract, if implemented

Inputs: immutable predecessor cohort and policy, source-readiness catalog, scoped negative knowledge, global root/exposure relations, current capacity/budgets, typed blocker/disposition census, and declared information cutoff. Output: a versioned closure/successor receipt that fixes source/question distribution and cumulative budget, or a bounded justified NOOP. It cannot mint new authority, erase exposure, change a frozen candidate, inspect protected outcomes, or schedule independently. Existing Control Plane publishes it and materializes at most the allowed work.

Positive-continuation obligations belong to existing Control Plane transitions: frozen source/protocol hashes, scope/authority, destination owner, idempotency key, expected receipt, deadline/unblock condition, and linked terminal result/PARK/BLOCK. A policy layer may observe that an obligation is unresolved; it may not override a refusal.

The learning metric is informative, distinct, admissible decisions per source/compute/time budget, conditional on access and continuation capacity. It is not raw proposal count, a semantic-novelty string, or one positive retrospective backtest. Adaptive allocation is deferred until a joined denominator exists; the first renewal can use fixed preregistered allocations.

## Why not a distinct program manager or source generator now?

Program management already belongs to research policy and Control Plane; generation already exists in documentary workers, policy callbacks and F. A second manager would add conflict over budgets, cohort identity and handoff ownership. There is no historical witness in which an extra reasoner, with the same sources and authority, demonstrably solves SEC403, missing paired DueBill prices, UST's absent task or Apple's economic-contract blockers. [Mandatory counterfactual](10_COUNTERFACTUAL_HISTORICAL_TEST.md).

This does not prohibit a later specialized research producer. A genuinely new source adapter or tool can be justified by a concrete unavailable input and an authorized source qualification. It should emit the existing source/proposal contracts rather than acquire lifecycle ownership.

## Reconsideration conditions

Revisit a separate subsystem only after: one target and authority policy are selected; continuation obligations reconcile; existing intake is measured; at least one successor cohort preserves exposure across a restart; and a controlled comparison identifies a function existing owners cannot correctly perform. Name that function and its exclusive outputs before approving a build.

A proposed layer must outperform a preregistered existing-policy baseline under matched source access and budget, improve distinct testable economic questions or independently confirmed downstream results, and preserve all controls. If gains disappear after deduplication or are explained by new paid data, there is no demonstrated architectural advantage. Public research does not settle that comparison; see [external challenge](07_EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_COMPARISON.md).
