## Concrete counterfactual answers

- **Duplicate frontier tasks:** earlier semantic root/source-aware admission and source-aware allocation could reduce repeated Apple work. Renaming tasks or raising issue caps cannot. The historical duplicate blocks should remain durable negative/exposure knowledge.
- **Qualified quant source:** **UNKNOWN**. Federation and source diversity policies offer a route for an available qualified packet, not proof that one exists. The ten missing-source refusals are not repaired by a new “intelligence” layer.
- **UST continuation:** a typed handoff reconciler would have changed the absence of a recorded next action. Actual execution remains conditional on scoped authority, compatible executor and screen/readiness contract. A scheduler defect is not established by a missing queued task.
- **Form144 SEC403:** a general orchestrator or agent would not bypass it. A compliant equivalent data route might; the historical result explicitly parks source acquisition before any economic attempt. Outcome-based retuning is forbidden.
- **Apple beyond integration:** **NO** on present evidence. Both actual five-stage challenges have twelve readiness blockers as well as no integration authority. Removing one authority label leaves substantive evidence deficits.
- **DueBill/LETF/ASML/Schedule13D:** better orchestration could materialize bounded data/design work if separately admissible. It cannot turn documented mechanics into measured edge, remove PIT requirements or make broad claims survive challenge.
- **VX economically tradable:** **NO**. The completed settlement expression, missing L2 execution/risk/capital data and not-yet-due prospective exits are different boundaries. A capital adapter or new research layer would not supply bid/ask/implementation evidence or independent alpha.
- **Four older positives:** a common denominator/lineage mapper could have improved preservation and follow-up visibility. This review does not recover enough downstream history to assert that they were dropped by automation rather than superseded, rejected or blocked. Their known exploratory configurations cannot be reset by reenrollment.

## Decision rule

Prefer the option that changes a proven process defect at the smallest existing seam: reconcile task-stage contracts and UST's next disposition; converge admission/identity controls; then renew a bounded source-qualified research cohort. Measure changed admissible transitions, duplicate exposure and source-qualified distinct roots before measuring throughput or positive counts. If all eligible next actions are accurately parked and a renewed admissible cohort yields ordinary scientific negatives, do not respond by weakening gates or inventing another subsystem.
