# Research → Capital: existing owners, missing continuity proof

**Verdict: MIXED, dominated by integration and authority; a narrow representation/transport contract is still required.** Research, Economic Identity, structural Portfolio Readiness, D0 lifecycle, Capital Desk, Book and Learning all exist. The inspected code does not provide a proven current-research-object route through all of them. That does not justify rebuilding those owners. It also does not prove that connecting the existing objects is configuration-only: the Desk's executable strategy language is narrower than the documentary/event/flow research language.

## Audit challenged by primary code and a fresh metadata probe

1. **There is already a C0→Economic→D0 seam.** `bind_scientific_economic_handoff` checks C0 verdict/posture/claim ceiling, candidate-generation hashes and W2 base, then `create_d0_shadow_from_scientific_handoff` preserves adjudication lineage. It explicitly owns no scheduler, outcome evaluator or capital decision. Saying the research-capital bridge is wholly absent would erase this code. Conversely, its test helper `w1_identity` manually constructs a qualified identity: passing those tests does not establish the production identity creation route. [W2 test helper and fixture seam](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/tests/test_wave2_c2_scientific_economic_d0_seam.py#L165).
2. **H L001 is still reproducible on W2.** On the existing readiness fixture W2 returns `READY_FOR_AUTHORIZED_ECONOMIC_TEST`, while downstream Economic Identity loses the declared instrument/universe/entry clock and changes the horizon from `one declared clock interval` to `one interval`. This is transmission failure in a fixture, not evidence that a real profitable strategy was stopped. The contract remains fail-closed for authority. [W2 composer at L213](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/research/research_os/expansion/economic_test_readiness_v1.py#L213), [original H probe](../audit/owner-recovery/20261026/pass-01/snapshots/horizontal_c1/research/research_os/architecture/horizontal_convergence1/probe_frozen_identity.py).
3. **Q repairs that specific fixture path.** The identical probe on Q preserves all four fields. The repair is gated on synthetic scope and an exact frozen candidate hash. It neither proves a nonsynthetic current-root path nor resolves Q B02 native/shared wire binding. Port/replay this behavior where appropriate; do not recreate it or claim Q is wholly qualified. [Q L217](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/4efadddf694eff0c0df00925b5b4b89194f32f8e/research/research_os/expansion/economic_test_readiness_v1.py#L217), [Q manifest](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/4efadddf694eff0c0df00925b5b4b89194f32f8e/POST_WAVE2_FINAL_QUALIFICATION_MANIFEST.md).
4. **Two Economic Identity shapes have not been shown equivalent.** The historical shared JSON schema requires string fields such as `hypothesis_generation`, `asset_class`, `beta_exposure`, `turnover`, and disallows additional properties. Native identity uses `hypothesis_id`, `generation_id`, `candidate_binding`, a candidate snapshot, field support/provenance and different exposure keys. Merely applying the old schema to the native record would reject it; merely renaming fields could lose unknown/conflicted/not-applicable states and provenance. A versioned, non-authorizing projection belongs at this boundary. [Shared schema](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/research/research_os/contracts/economic-identity.schema.json), [native structural representation](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/research/research_os/expansion/portfolio_v2.py#L264).
5. **D0 shadow Book is not a position ledger.** `shadow_book_state` says `IDENTITY_LIFECYCLE_ONLY_NO_POSITIONS`; `create_shadow_contract` requires an external evidence evaluator and keeps observation false. The Capital Desk separately chooses its CAPITAL or EVALUATION ledger from StrategyDefinition state. Treating these two uses of “shadow” as the same integrated Book would be a category error. [D0 L486](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/research/research_os/expansion/_economic_lifecycle_d0.py#L486), [Desk L61](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/src/quant/desk/desk.py#L61).
6. **Desk integration is not universally just a wire.** `StrategySpec`/`weights_for` supports cross-sectional trailing-return momentum/reversal on PricePanel; the UST protocol is a mechanism screen, while event entitlements and futures expressions have other clocks and cashflow semantics. Never squeeze them into the existing cross-sectional signal and call it continuity. First choose one admissible executable expression; add only its missing adapter under the existing Factory/Desk owners. A general event/futures execution framework is not yet justified. [StrategySpec L23 and weights_for L149](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/src/quant/factory/signals.py#L23).
7. **There is real historical research→evaluation→Learning machinery.** V1 `_finish` registers falsified strategies on the evaluation track and accepted ones into its historical lifecycle. This rebuts “Book/Learning is only a diagram”, but its automatic SHADOW transition is not a license to port that behavior into the currently closed modern authority context. [V1 registration in W2](https://github.com/fahimahmedb/Quant-Trade/blob/ebc72b9fd14f91420c37e0b7607f9bfb584a289a/src/quant/factory/workers.py#L217).

## Smallest contract to close the seam

Under the existing Economic/Portfolio/Control/Desk owners, require one immutable transport envelope containing: root/proposal identity, trial/epoch and prior-exposure references, hypothesis/generation/candidate hash, frozen protocol/experiment/result/adjudication hashes and claim ceiling, native Economic Identity hash with typed field provenance, Portfolio Readiness hash, implementation/version and instrument/cashflow/clock semantics, observation identity/start boundary, evaluation-only entitlement and explicit authority scope, and destination receipt/idempotency key. The receiving owner must either emit a linked receipt or a reasoned PARK/BLOCK with an unblock predicate. A transport object may reference outcomes but cannot let a pre-outcome owner inspect them or upgrade claims.

This is a **small adapter/obligation contract**, not a new research engine, Capital Desk or Book. Reuse D0's binding and evidence interfaces, `StrategyDefinition`, DeskJournal, the EVALUATION ledger, existing Learning/Memory. The exact adapter cannot be specified fully until a real eligible expression is selected; UNKNOWN is preferable to fabricating generic execution compatibility.

Qualification sequence: reproduce L001, port the scoped Q repair with both positive and unknown/conflict cases, prove a lossless native/shared projection, replay one real frozen research object through every available metadata stage (accept a justified block), then run a synthetic evaluation witness through Desk/Book/Feedback with the same identity envelope. An actual new historical outcome look or prospective acquisition remains subject to its own economic/observation authority. No real-capital or live-trading grant is needed for a synthetic evaluation witness or metadata transport proof. Those are separate future decisions.
