# Final decision digest

**Build less, converge selectively, and close the policy/continuation seams.** Quant already contains generation, admission, discovery, freezing, validation, economic/lifecycle contracts and a persistent Desk/Book/Learning path. It lacks a proven, consistently identified operational chain across those pieces. The immediate objective is a lawful informative next action and durable disposition, not another batch of idea names.

PRIMARY_CURRENT_BOTTLENECK= Fragmented, incompletely converged execution of legitimate next actions: old operational runtime versus newer qualified components, bounded issuance without a governed successor transaction, and incomplete durable continuation/identity transport. This is the strongest actionable diagnosis, not a quantified causal estimate of lost alpha.

SECONDARY_BOTTLENECKS= Admissible source/data availability; scope/authority conflicts and old task-stage contracts; repeated narrow packets; incomplete joined process denominator; native/shared Economic Identity and executable-expression transport. Some stops are scientifically correct and should remain stops.

IS_IDEA_GENERATION_THE_PRIMARY_PROBLEM= **NO on present evidence.** Real generation exists; additional economically distinct, testable ideas may still be needed. The evidence does not show another generator is the best intervention.

IS_FRONTIER_RENEWAL_MISSING= **PARTIAL.** Config, source-aware allocation, cohort labels/budgets and cross-cohort dedup primitives exist. A qualified immutable predecessor→successor policy transaction with cumulative exposure/budget and adaptation boundaries is not recovered. Current 4×12 quotas are exhausted; scientific search-space exhaustion is not proven. W2's pure refill probe returns `NOOP_SEARCH_SPACE_INCOMPLETE` and still finds two unused qualified flow packets.

IS_POSITIVE_CONTINUATION_BROKEN= **PARTIAL.** Apple producer→five-stage challenge is real. Form144 reached source acquisition and parked at SEC403. UST has a protocol and post-freeze declaration but no recovered execution task/result and a missing validation receipt. This supports an incomplete next-disposition seam; **a scheduler defect is UNKNOWN**, and UST authority/claim scope must be reconciled first. No general replacement orchestrator is justified.

IS_RESEARCH_TO_CAPITAL_MISSING= **MIXED: integration dominant, authority and a narrow transport/representation gap.** Existing scientific→Economic→D0 binding, Desk, evaluation ledger, Book and Learning are implemented/tested. No current 15-root complete chain is established. D0 lifecycle shadow state is not positions; Desk's existing signal language cannot silently express every event/futures protocol. Metadata/synthetic evaluation proof requires no live-trading permission; real outcome acquisition retains its separate membrane.

IS_ADVANCED_RESEARCH_REQUIRED= **THIN_LAYER only**, inside existing research policy/Control. A distinct subsystem is NOT_YET justified. Its candidate function is lawful program renewal; it must not become a second intake, Discovery, memory, adjudicator or scheduler.

WHAT_EXISTING_CAPABILITIES_SHOULD_BE_REUSED= W2 RI/source planner/replenisher/Discovery/Factory/C0/D0; existing Control/Oracle; selected V3 admission and F root/exposure/federation primitives; latest C0 and forward controls; Q's applicable repairs; H/V longitudinal attacks; actual source dispositions; existing Desk/Book/Learning/Memory.

WHAT_EXISTING_CAPABILITIES_SHOULD_NOT_BE_REBUILT= Idea-generating agent fleet, general Discovery Engine, protocol freezer, independent validator, research scheduler, source registry, Capital Desk, persistent Book or second scientific memory. No replacement is proved necessary.

WHAT_MUST_BE_CONVERGED= Exact target/gate/release identities; relevant C0/forward integrity semantics; one proposal admission write owner and root/exposure mapping if intake is resumed; native/shared identity transport; actual off-rail continuation dispositions. This is selective semantic convergence, not merge-everything.

WHAT_MUST_BE_REPAIRED= Missing typed protocol→next-disposition materialization/receipt where established; old flow task/action mismatch with lawful reenrollment; frozen metadata propagation; C0 confirmation counting; applicable composed integrity checks; downstream receipt/denominator joins. Some items already have qualified source repairs to port and replay.

WHAT_IS_TRULY_NEW= Thin versioned cohort closure/successor decision; explicit cross-owner continuation obligation/disposition contract; lossless ID/authority/exposure mapping and derived denominator; a single expression transport adapter only when a real eligible expression demonstrates need. Existing primitives supply most implementation. A general productivity optimizer and new Advanced Research runtime are deferred.

MINIMUM_ARCHITECTURE_DELTA= **A few contracts under existing owners:** (1) preserve one root/protocol/exposure identity across intake, freeze, next-task/disposition and economic transport; (2) renew a bounded program through an immutable source-ready successor policy without resetting history. First reconcile integrity/authority and demonstrate one lawful positive continuation or explicit block. Then prove one bounded successor. Add only the missing expression adapter when selected research reaches that boundary.

WHY_THIS_DELTA_IS_SMALLER_THAN_THE_ALTERNATIVES= It repairs arrows and policy transitions instead of replacing nodes. Convergence alone leaves finite cohorts capped; renewal alone emits more blocked work; continuation alone leaves future inventory finite; a new manager duplicates all three owners. F/V3/Q are reused by function and scope rather than bulk-integrated as a new architecture.

EXPECTED_CHANGE_TO_REAL_RESEARCH_OUTCOMES= Fewer repeated Apple/source-block tasks; fewer silent handoff losses; UST becomes a linked explicit scope/source block or an authorized frozen-screen task; off-rail parks inform the next search; unused source-ready questions can be attempted within a new bounded cohort. SEC403, missing DueBill/LETF data, absent independent evidence, Apple's readiness blockers and VX execution/OOS constraints remain unless separately resolved. Better economic discoveries and net alpha remain **UNKNOWN**, not promised benefits.

## Measurement plan

Baseline: runtime snapshot `16eafd…` has 72 tasks (34 COMPLETE, 38 BLOCKED), 15 frozen hypotheses and 14 completed documentary validation tasks. Block partition: 18 duplicates, 10 missing quant source, 9 stage-scope mismatch, 1 epoch-context block. There is one Form144 source-acquisition execution on a separate rail and no recovered completed costed economic test among the 15. These are scoped counts, not a complete historical denominator or an acceptance rate.

1. **Engineering closure:** every eligible handoff has one linked next task/result or explicit PARK/BLOCK with owner and unblock predicate; unresolved obligations and latency are visible. Duplicate delivery/restart does not double execute or reset exposure. All identity/frozen metadata fields survive or remain typed UNKNOWN. Mandatory applicable integrity gates pass on the exact assembled target.
2. **Bounded renewal:** exactly one preregistered successor with cumulative budget/exposure; same-root cosmetic changes stay duplicates. Measure source-ready distinct questions and informative dispositions per source/compute/time budget; report unavailable-source time, capacity limits and denied authority separately. Zero emitted tasks is acceptable when none is lawful/useful.
3. **Denominators:** attempted generation (including discarded drafts), admitted/rejected proposals, Candidates, frozen protocols, source/infrastructure attempts, actual outcome looks/variants, scientific/economic positives, parks, kills, duplicate relations and data blocks joined by immutable IDs. Historical unknowns and fixture counts stay outside production totals.
4. **Economic evidence later:** when separately authorized and data-ready, assess protected OOS/replication, costs/implementability, capacity, factor/beta exposure, independent confirmation and incremental portfolio usefulness. Never choose policy on idea volume or best retrospective result. A finite campaign may be underpowered to rank policies; report UNKNOWN.

FAILURE_CRITERIA= Any authority/PIT/outcome/identity/exposure regression fails immediately. Stop expansion if successor work merely repeats known blocks, eligible continuation still silently disappears, required hostile failures are unexplained, or improvements vanish after root/variant deduplication. If targeted recovery already supplies valid dispositions and equivalent controls, omit the corresponding repair. If no genuine source-ready question remains, PARK and solve the concrete source dependency; do not build a larger generator. Failure of a preregistered, sufficiently informative economic criterion can falsify the specified pilot benefit. Otherwise no-edge remains inconclusive and may justify a budget stop; it does not falsify an entire opportunity space.

## Top five Owner decisions

1. Select the concrete target and mandatory qualification/promotion gates; distinguish it from installed Oracle and the routing default.
2. Resolve UST's scoped grant versus global denial, and whether the frozen CMT screen is the intended bounded question. Keep the original freeze/exposure immutable.

4. Select one proposal admission contract and identity/exposure mapping authority; reject duplicate active write ownership across F/V3/legacy intake.
5. Define the next evidence milestone: a real linked disposition, one safe successor, and metadata/synthetic evaluation transport first; separately authorize any later outcome/observation pilot. No real-capital/live-trading request is implied.
