# EXTERNAL RESEARCH CHALLENGE

External research was performed only after repository reconstruction and blind-vs-prior comparison. Literature is used to challenge desired properties, never to infer repository state.

## Autonomous scientific agents

REPOSITORY_FACT=Quant has substantial agentic/research components, but one canonical task-to-scientific-sink autonomous lineage is not yet proven.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=Recent agentic-science literature emphasizes end-to-end traceability, reproducible reporting, structured experimental execution and explicit evaluation rather than equating agent count with trustworthy discovery. A 2026 Nature Reviews Methods Primers review identifies memory, tool use, task decomposition and communication as foundations while emphasizing reproducible evaluation/reporting and risks such as hallucination/tool misuse. The 2026 peer-reviewed AI Scientist demonstrates end-to-end computational research but uses explicit experiment/replication structure and a fixed experimental protocol for its evaluation.

INFERENCE=Do not build another agent fleet to solve an unproven join. First prove provenance and execution continuity of the existing pieces. Autonomous paper/research production is not equivalent to scientifically verified or economically useful quant discovery.

Sources:
- Nature Reviews Methods Primers (2026), `Agentic AI systems for scientific discovery`, https://www.nature.com/articles/s43586-026-00534-y
- Nature (2026), `Towards end-to-end automation of AI research`, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10265-5
- Nature Computational Science (2026), `Provenance grounds trust in autonomous science`, https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-026-01035-4

## Preregistration and adaptive experimentation

REPOSITORY_FACT=The selected first witness is already prospectively frozen; historical lineages carry varying exposure/contamination.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=Preregistration separates planned confirmatory work from data-dependent exploration. Registered Reports go further by evaluating methods before results and accepting rigorous negative/null outcomes. Adaptive methods do not erase the need to predeclare information sets, decision rules and multiplicity handling.

INFERENCE=Preserve frozen estimand, source, PIT, analysis, cost screen, decision rule and stop conditions before protected outcomes. Any outcome-informed change creates exploratory/new lineage. A failed/park/null result remains a valid durable result.

Sources:
- Center for Open Science, Preregistration, https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg
- Center for Open Science, Registered Reports, https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports
- Scientific Reports Registered Reports policy, https://www.nature.com/srep/journal-policies/registered-reports

## Provenance and reproducible workflows

REPOSITORY_FACT=Quant already uses immutable IDs, hashes, receipts, source records and multiple ledgers, but some cross-stage joins and historical denominator coverage remain incomplete.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=W3C PROV models provenance explicitly through entities, activities and agents, including generation, use, derivation, plans and delegation. FAIR principles require persistent identification, rich metadata, provenance and reusable/machine-actionable research objects and explicitly extend to algorithms/tools/workflows.

INFERENCE=The required delta is not a new Scientific Memory or generic data platform. Preserve exact entity/activity/agent lineage across experiment, source receipt, code/environment, evaluator, result and memory. UNKNOWN historical coverage must not be fabricated.

Sources:
- W3C PROV-O Recommendation, https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/
- Wilkinson et al., `The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship`, Scientific Data (2016), https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618

## Negative-result preservation and scientific memory

REPOSITORY_FACT=Quant already records parks, blocks, duplicates and negative memory, but complete historical denominators are not recovered.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=Registered-report practice removes result sign from publication/acceptance incentives, and open-science lifecycle guidance treats durable linked plans, data, code and outcomes as part of the research record.

INFERENCE=Do not optimize the system for positive-result throughput. Preserve `PARK_DATA`, authority blocks, nulls, kills, duplicate admissions, failed source attempts and deviations separately from scientific negatives. Denominators must append across retries/cohorts.

## Multiple testing and quant replication

REPOSITORY_FACT=Quant has many historical hypotheses/versions and incomplete total historical denominator/exposure. The selected first witness is deliberately narrow and bounded.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=Harvey, Liu and Zhu show that extensive factor search raises the evidentiary threshold under multiple testing; Bailey/López de Prado and related work show selection bias/backtest overfitting can inflate performance. These methods are design-dependent and their numerical thresholds are not universal.

INFERENCE=Record every materially distinct tested variant, prior exposure and selection step. Do not reset multiplicity with a new cohort/name. Do not install a universal t-statistic or DSR threshold as architecture law; select the method appropriate to the eventual experiment family.

Sources:
- Harvey, Liu & Zhu, RFS 2016, https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/29/1/5/1843824
- Bailey & López de Prado, `The Deflated Sharpe Ratio`, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551
- Bailey et al., `The Probability of Backtest Overfitting`, https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253

## Transaction costs, capacity and portfolio evaluation

REPOSITORY_FACT=The selected witness has explicit screening cost assumptions, but no repository evidence yet proves real execution-cost calibration, capacity or incremental portfolio value for it.

EXTERNAL_PROPERTY=Novy-Marx and Velikov show transaction costs materially change anomaly profitability and capacity, with turnover central to implementability. Strategy approval and portfolio usefulness are downstream questions from discovering a statistical relation.

INFERENCE=The first bounded scientific result may use its frozen declared cost screen, but economic validation must later obtain venue/instrument-specific spread/fee/depth/impact/funding evidence and evaluate incremental portfolio contribution. Scientific validity must not be upgraded into economic or capital readiness.

Sources:
- Novy-Marx & Velikov, RFS 2016, `A Taxonomy of Anomalies and Their Trading Costs`, https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/29/1/104/1844518
- Bailey, López de Prado & del Pozo, `The Strategy Approval Decision`, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2003638
