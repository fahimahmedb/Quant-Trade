# Required preflight before any real source access

All gates G01–G12 in the machine-readable matrix must close for the concrete successor. They are independently derived requirements, not an Owner gate list. “Not demonstrated” is not “failed mechanism.” This audit provides no permission to close them by accessing a live source.

G01 — Identity and information set: commit unique trial/generation/parent identities and immutable source/code/environment/protocol hashes. Enumerate who has seen which documents, metadata, source bodies, labels, returns and past results, at what times. Include private-history UNKNOWN, exposure cutoff, allowed discovery material, closed outcomes and reviewer access rules. Separate trial declaration from source/result access.

G02 — Scientific preregistration: bind mechanism, payer conjecture, universe, expression, direction, lag, horizon, schedule/start rule, sample window, exclusions, missingness, revision and survivorship policies, cost/capacity assumptions, benchmark, primary statistic, adequacy and exact decision map. For the proposed direct successor, freeze 21 scheduled slots 5s apart, 19 possible lagged intervals; >=12 valid nonzero signals and >=60s source span. No favorable-window choice after access. Define lateness/missed-slot behavior without catch-up bursts, and total-window funding exclusion, including day boundaries.

G03 — Source legitimacy: qualify the exact endpoint/version/fields, permission and allowed research use from existing official documentation and synthetic contract examples. A 200 response or Swagger listing is insufficient. Identify host/network policy, rate/budget, account requirements and all redirects; deny alternate endpoints/fallbacks. Historical missing BBO requires explicit resolution before source authority. If offline documentation cannot resolve availability, a separately authorized, preregistered source-qualification attempt is itself exposed and accounted for; it is not a free preflight peek or automatically the economic trial.

G04 — PIT: required finite positive BBO and sizes, bid<=ask, symbol/schema correct; retain source timestamp, UTC send/receive and monotonic clocks, synchronization uncertainty and provenance. Source age <=10s and future tolerance <=1s are historical design bounds, not proof of tradability. Adjacent source timestamps strictly increase with gaps 1..12s. Signal must be known before entry; delays/caching/duplicate payloads cannot be treated as fills. Hash raw bytes at acquisition; trace every admitted/rejected interval. No outcome-derived selection.

G05 — Acquisition: explicit required-field errors (no assert/truthiness dependence), first non-200/malformed/oversize stop, max 21 requests, 3s request timeout, <16384B accepted body, <=120s process and <=2MiB total budget, zero retries. Bound transport, redirects, DNS/network failures and clock jumps; external supervisor must enforce the deadline even if inner code catches exceptions. Durable intent before first request, idempotent exclusive reservation, restart cannot reacquire; partial files and interrupted attempts remain immutable. No secondary collector.

G06 — Execution/math: synthetic fixtures with known expected numbers verify sign uses i-1, entry i, exit i+1, adverse ask/bid direction, entry-mid denominator and unit conversion. Central fees 5bp per side +1bp extra total (11bp), stress 10bp/side +1bp (21bp), crossing separately observed. Zero signal abstains. Funding-boundary window parks; inverse XBTUSD linear approximation limits claims. Perfect-direction absolute next-mid move minus 11bp is an unattainable upper bound, not a second selected strategy. Do not imply fills or capital ROI from this proxy.

G07 — Decision and failure law: adequate data plus mean central<=0 or every eligible favorable bound<=0 supports only the exact sampled-expression negative. Positive requires central mean>=5bp, stress positive and both predefined chronological halves positive; otherwise PARK. Freeze how halves use original chronological slots versus valid trades. Insufficient/invalid data parks with no economic verdict. An access, interruption, capture repair, schema change or changed decision map cannot silently reopen the same ID.

G08 — Denominator: append trial/exposure registration before access and durable receipt for every attempted run, including zero-valid/failed runs. Freeze family membership, prior exposures, attempted variants, one expression, one diagnostic bound, two cost scenarios and dependent intervals. Effective trials UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. No fabricated multiplicity correction or nominal significance.

G09 — Authority: separately signed/scoped declarations for trial, source read, execution, result access, receipt write and exposure append. Bind actor, identities, exact limits, validity window, revocation and failure actions. Scientific memory writes require another explicit grant. No chaining implicit permissions.

G10 — Engineering baseline: require all evidence in 04 before source authority is exercised: exact executable product tree/environment, complete negative and positive synthetic checks, composed path verification and immutable qualification logs. A generic dry-run READY or green SHA does not close this gate.

G11 — Custody and review: append-only raw/receipt/result storage with content digests, completion/partial status and qualified recomputation semantics; isolate closed outcomes, audit summaries and production science from the runner. Independent reviewer checks frozen package and code before exposure; after access, accept all dispositions without retuning. Review provenance/assertions need authentication beyond self-generated hashes.

G12 — Claims and stop: freeze exploratory-only language, no long-run expectancy/edge/capital/autonomy/independent-confirmation claim. Positive outcome warrants a separately declared nonoverlapping replication, not deployment. One bounded run ends regardless of outcome. No memory/autonomy replay follows without its own scope. Preserve failure and stop if any gate changes after freeze.
