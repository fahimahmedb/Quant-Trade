A first adequate negative can falsify the declared cost hurdle in that sample, within approximation limits; it cannot reject the whole mechanism or smaller effects elsewhere. A data failure says nothing about economic sign. A first adequate positive is exploratory support for another separately frozen test. Nonoverlap alone does not establish full independence: shared model choice, outcome access, source, market regime, reviewer context and selection history must be assessed.

Independent confirmation requires new admissible evidence not selected from the original result, frozen analysis before access, authenticated reviewer/producer separation and explicit semantic/cognitive/retrieval independence. Recomputing the same artifact, moving a hash through another validator or asking a second wrapper to review it is not an independent empirical result.
