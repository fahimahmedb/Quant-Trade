
## 3. What this does not claim

It does not shorten `sqrt(T)`. It raises `IR` through frequency, lowers the
marginal cost of evidence, and starts real execution learning at a size whose
downside is second-order. Qualification of any lane remains on its own
pre-registered protocol, and `FORM4_CONFIRMATORY_V1` remains frozen.
