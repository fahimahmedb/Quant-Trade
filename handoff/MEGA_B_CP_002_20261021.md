## Adversarial law implemented

The following are fail-closed Discovery blockers rather than favorable interpretations:

- statistical signal without mechanism;
- causal story without payer;
- document change without qualified causal transfer (`DOCUMENT_CHANGE != EDGE`);
- good fundamentals without expectation/mispricing basis (`GOOD_FUNDAMENTALS != MISPRICING`);
- rule existence without actual forced action (`RULE_EXISTS != FORCED_FLOW`);
- undefined universe or tradable expression;
- missing / inconsistent source clocks;
- entry before source availability/effectiveness;
- explicit identity ambiguity;
- unbounded expected costs or capacity hypothesis;
- incomplete mechanism/economic exposure identity;
- same mechanism under different labels;
- same economic exposure under a different wrapper;
- unknown identity being promoted to novelty;
- outcome-shaped payloads entering Discovery, including alpha, PnL, return series, effect size, market response and price response.

Lexical similarity and empirical correlation are not used as substitutes for structural identity.
