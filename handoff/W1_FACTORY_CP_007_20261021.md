## WHAT_GAP_WAS_PROVEN

At CP006, the current economic-readiness composer built a W1 Experiment Infrastructure attestation but the canonical Validator did not decide that law itself. The producer called `validator_economic_readiness_v3()` and then post-mutated the returned Validator receipt by inserting an `experiment_infrastructure` check and potentially rewriting status/readiness/claim ceiling.
