## Interpretation rules

- `RATIFIED`: explicit Owner decision.
- `PARTIALLY_RATIFIED`: Owner fixed part of the decision and left named fields unresolved.
- `STRUCTURALLY_RATIFIED`: architecture/principle ratified; numerical/operational details remain unresolved.
- `OWNER_OVERRIDE`: explicit exception to a prior rule.
- `DELEGATED_IMPLEMENTATION`: engineering choice accepted for the current implementation, not permanent Owner product doctrine.
- `DRAFT_OR_SUPPORT_ONLY`: no Owner authority.
- absence of an Owner decision does not imply permission.

No entry in this registry opens economic, prospective, real-capital or live-trading authority unless an explicit later Owner artifact says so.
