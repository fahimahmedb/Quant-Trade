## 3. Corporate-action calibration grouping

Two calibration identifiers now have distinct roles:

- `CALIBRATION_SECURITY_LINEAGE_ID` = coherent security/listing lineage through ordinary identity transitions;
- `CALIBRATION_DEPENDENCE_GROUP_ID` = connected corporate-reorganization dependence component across mergers, successors, spin-offs/separations and other authorized material linkage.

Ordinary stock/reverse splits do not by themselves create a new calibration lineage.

Unresolved corporate-action linkage emits:

`CALIBRATION_DEPENDENCE_GROUP_UNRESOLVED`

and affected ambiguous objects are excluded from q/window calibration rather than guessed.

## 4. Split and resampling unit

Q-selection and window-selection are disjoint by `CALIBRATION_DEPENDENCE_GROUP_ID`.

The q stability/resampling procedure also uses the dependence group as the outer resampling cluster.

The existing equal-total-mass-per-security loss weighting remains a separate downstream aggregation rule; it is not the split or bootstrap unit.

## 5. Split-capacity design

The split is intentionally asymmetric:

- q split = minimum ex-ante sufficient independent dependence groups required by the frozen q-estimability/stability protocol;
- window split = all residual eligible dependence groups subject to its own frozen minimum coverage requirement.

A default symmetric 50/50 split is not controlling.

The q candidate set / estimator / clustered-resampling / stability threshold / minimum-group rule must be frozen **before** deriving the q split capacity.

Then derive:

`N_Q_REQUIRED`

or equivalent deterministic pre-result design requirement.

If the eligible corpus cannot satisfy both q and window requirements:

`CALIBRATION_SPLIT_CAPACITY_UNRESOLVED`.

No post-result split reallocation is authorized.
