### Concrete failures and localization

1. Replay trusted self-consistent hashes without reapplying parts of the freeze
   contract. The producer rejected invalid observed attempt counts and missing
   declarations, while reconstructed replay admitted them. Embedded/outer version,
   execution mode, capability and partial frozen/legacy state could contradict
   the claimed frozen artifact. Root: admission checks lived only in plan creation
   or only one representation marker.
2. Duplicate protocol/code/environment/seed/source-route identities in the plan
   and frozen body were never compared. Rehashing each body/receipt could preserve
   both contradictory claims. Python numeric equality also equates integer and
   float seeds whose serialized identities differ. Root: per-body hashes were
   checked without enforcing duplicate-field agreement.
3. Replay and rebinding reread mutable caller plans after checking them. A
   deterministic pause after receipt checking changed the returned experiment ID;
   swapping the caller plan after replay could make a changed source match the
   replacement. Root: the reference being checked was not captured at entry.

Narrow repairs: replay requires the existing declarations and valid observed
