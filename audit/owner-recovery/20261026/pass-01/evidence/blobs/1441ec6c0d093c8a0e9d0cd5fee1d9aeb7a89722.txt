## Regression failure classification

- Lane-local regression: return to that port; do not continue stacking ports.
- Seam regression: classify producer/consumer ownership before changing code.
- Shared schema/version conflict: canonical integrator adjudicates; siblings remain unchanged.
- Global truth conflict: integrator-owned resolution only.
- Authority or outcome violation: immediate hard blocker; do not qualify around it.
- External missing dependency: `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`, never falsify PASS.
