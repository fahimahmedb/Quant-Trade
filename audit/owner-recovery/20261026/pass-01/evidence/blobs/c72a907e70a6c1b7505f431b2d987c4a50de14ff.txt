## Concrete failures and root causes

The 56 new fixtures produce **43 failures / 13 passes** against the exact previous
watchdog module, with the final fixture retained. Three grouped repair items:

1. Unqualified timestamps: malformed/naive starts raised exceptions, aborting the
   whole health inspection and hiding other worker/crash/starvation faults.
   Future timestamps were accepted as healthy despite no causal start/heartbeat
   proof. Missing starts alone were already conservative; malformed proof was not.
2. Unqualified durations: NaN/infinity and bool aliases could report a healthy
   worker or suppress missing-heartbeat detection. Type/overflow errors also
   interrupted inspection. A bad timeout configuration lacked an explicit error.
3. Exact expiry: age equal to its lease/heartbeat duration was treated as still
   live. A zero duration consequently did not expire a current timestamp.

The failures concern truth inside the existing watchdog, not a lost timestamp
between producer/consumer modules. No actual worker or observation is executed.

## Minimal semantic repair

A local elapsed-time helper accepts aware timestamps and observer clocks, compares
UTC instants and returns unknown for invalid, timezone-ambiguous or future proof.
A lease is stale when time/duration cannot prove health, or age reaches expiry.
Malformed heartbeat proof emits existing HEARTBEAT_MISSING and inspection continues
through other existing alert classes. Its detail remains text, including malformed
wire values. Lease duration and heartbeat timeout require finite nonnegative
int/float values, excluding bool aliases and overflow. Missing lease duration
retains the existing 600-second default. Invalid monitor timeout configuration
raises ValueError explicitly; it cannot return an apparently healthy inspection.

Compatibility: expiry equality now alerts; zero duration is valid immediate
expiry. Ordinary positive durations, UTC/offset spellings and healthy workers
retain their behavior. Existing alert codes/wire shape remain unchanged. This is
a conservative diagnostic; it does not revoke leases, retry work or authorize it.
