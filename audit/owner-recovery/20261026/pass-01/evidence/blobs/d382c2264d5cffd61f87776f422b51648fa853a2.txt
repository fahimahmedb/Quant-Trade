# F2 — primary data-access decision

DECISION = BLOCKED_DATA_PERMISSION. Do not acquire F2 market/candle/outcome
data from Kalshi's API or website under the current free, credential-free
research scope. No account, paid licence or permission request is initiated.
This is an access decision, not an empirical rejection of favourite bias.

The [official developer-agreement page](https://kalshi.com/developer-agreement)
links the [v1.1 PDF](https://assets.kalshi.com/Kalshi-Developer-Agreement.pdf).
Sections 3/3.1 limit API use and stored data to the member's own trading;
section 4.1 requires exchange membership, agreement acceptance and contact
information. Public endpoint HTTP 200 responses do not establish those rights.

The same official page links the
[Data Terms of Use](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/kalshi-data-terms-of-service.pdf).
Its permitted-use section excludes software development from unconsented
non-commercial use; its prohibited-use section restricts systematic extraction
and AI-related use. The website is therefore not an alternative acquisition
route for this project.

Both linked primary PDFs were downloaded normally, read and SHA256-recorded
in `primary_terms_2026-10-09.json`. Legal PDFs/text remain outside git; no
market or settlement record was acquired. Earlier metadata probes are already
recorded in the shared terms note. No outcome look or statistical budget is
consumed by this decision.
