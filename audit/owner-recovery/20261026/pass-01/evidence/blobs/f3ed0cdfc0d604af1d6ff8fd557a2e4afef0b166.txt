Module: `src/quant/desk/journal.py`. Existing capability: immutable decision-time
snapshots and `begin(plan) -> apply -> commit(outcome)` recovery, preserving the
original decision rather than re-deciding against a partly filled Book.
North Star §§7, 10, 12. Starting depth: D2 plus whole-Desk interrupted-session
tests; same-object failed writes and competing journal writers unqualified.

Defects/root causes/repairs:

1. Replayed begin overwrote pending intent; begin after completion resurrected
   pending work. Identical pending replay is now a no-op; conflicting intent and
   completed-opportunity reopening raise `JournalConflict`.
2. Completion could remove intent under a different strategy/session or claim
   NO_TRADE despite pending fills. Bind completion/ticket identity, fill payload
   when supplied, and BOOKED/rebalanced semantics before clearing intent.
3. Caller-owned ticket/fill/snapshot containers and returned views aliased frozen
   internal records. Copy at admission and read boundaries; outcomes are copied.
4. Failed writes advanced in-memory processed/snapshot membership and could
   suppress retry. Restore actual durable state on all save failures, including
   acknowledgement loss after publication.
5. Atomic replacement alone allowed stale journal writers to erase processed
   identities. Compare the complete observed document under a stable file lock;
   refresh and raise a conflict for caller reconsideration.
6. Out-of-order completion moved per-strategy rebalance recency backward; unknown
   terminal states became processed sessions. Preserve maximum recency and
   require the existing OpportunityTicket terminal-state contract.

Adversaries: failure before/after write for begin/commit/session-open/session-close;
changed nested intent under the same identity; terminal reopening; contradictory
completion; caller/view mutation; two concurrent stale writers and explicit retry;
out-of-order completion; UNKNOWN terminal status.
