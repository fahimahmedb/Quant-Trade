## Challenge — queue task identity and candidate identity are different objects

A first interpretation of the historical repair was:

`NO_IDEA_ENTERS_QUEUE_WITHOUT_NOVELTY_EVIDENCE`.

Deep comparison with current canonical shows that this is too strict and would reintroduce unnecessary governance.

Current architecture intentionally distinguishes:

1. a **frontier/generation task**, which may exist before a concrete economic identity has been generated;
2. a **candidate hypothesis**, whose mechanism/entity/family identity is concrete enough to compare against current and legacy scientific memory;
3. a **frozen hypothesis**, which is durable input to later validation.

`research/research_os/research_refill_policy.py` explicitly states:
- frontier is a generation mandate, not a candidate;
- unknown identity is not novelty;
- Scientific Memory matching is deferred until candidate identity is resolved;
- no novelty claim is made at the frontier-task stage.

This is the correct simplification: the scheduler does not need semantic novelty proof for an object whose scientific identity does not yet exist.

Therefore the durable modern law is NOT "screen before any task creation".

It is:

`ALLOW_BOUNDED_GENERATION_TASK_WITH_UNRESOLVED_IDENTITY`

followed by

`SCREEN_CONCRETE_CANDIDATE_BEFORE_FREEZE_OR_VALIDATION_HANDOFF`.

This prevents duplicate science without forcing every scheduler/refill action to perform impossible or premature semantic archaeology.
