# L0 Research Seed — US Trump Policy Event Transmission

Status: IDEA_GENERATED only. This is a causal research seed under `EDGE_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md`; it is not a directional market call and it does not authorize an economic look by itself.

## Question

Can genuinely surprising policy actions, announcements, threats, reversals, executive actions, tariff moves, sanctions, immigration measures, fiscal proposals, or pressure on monetary-policy institutions associated with Donald Trump create repeatable delayed transmission, overreaction, or cross-asset dislocations after controlling for information that was already priced?

## L0 fields

- `MECHANISM`: event-driven information diffusion / policy uncertainty / cross-asset repricing.
- `WHO_PAYS`: participants that react slowly, hedge mechanically, rebalance after confirmation, or initially misclassify the policy shock.
- `WHY_PERSIST`: heterogeneous interpretation, legal/implementation uncertainty, confirmation lags, fragmented positioning, differing market hours, and slow transmission from first-order to second-order exposures.
- `CHEAP_DATA_NOW`: public timestamped White House / executive / agency releases, official statements, tariff/sanctions notices, Congressional/public documents, and free market/reference data sufficient for documentary L0 source qualification.
- `CHEAP_EXPRESSION`: candidate relative expressions only, such as sector/industry vs broad index, US vs non-US peers, FX/rates/commodities linked to the policy channel, or first-order vs second-order beneficiaries/losers. No expression is selected before L0 triage.
- `FATAL_PROBLEM`: most headline information may be pre-announced, leaked, already priced, timestamp-ambiguous, or confounded by simultaneous macro news; a narrative that cannot isolate genuine surprise must be killed or parked.

## Required L0 discipline

1. Distinguish `information already priced` from `new surprise` using the earliest public timestamp available.
2. Prefer official primary sources and events with an objectively identifiable release/announcement time.
3. Search for disconfirming cases where the same class of announcement produced no delayed effect or an opposite effect.
4. Do not inspect signal-to-future-return/PnL outcomes during L0.
5. Do not tune event classes, windows, assets, or thresholds after reading outcomes.
6. Do not reopen a killed narrative under a new label.
7. Respect the global one-economic-look invariant and every already-frozen prospective.

## Candidate causal channels to triage

- tariffs / trade restrictions → exporters, importers, industrial inputs, FX and rates;
- sanctions / geopolitical restrictions → commodities, shipping, defense, country/sector exposures;
- immigration / labor policy → labor-sensitive industries, inflation/rates expectations;
- fiscal / tax policy → rates, sector earnings, domestic-vs-international exposures;
- regulatory/executive policy → directly affected sectors vs peers;
- public pressure around Fed policy → rates/FX/financial conditions, only if a clean surprise timestamp exists.
