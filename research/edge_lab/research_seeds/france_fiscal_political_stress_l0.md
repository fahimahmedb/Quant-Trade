# L0 Research Seed — France fiscal/political stress transmission

Status: `IDEA_GENERATED`
Exposure class: no economic outcome read for this seed
Lane: Discovery / L0 only
Owner intent: add a macro research direction without changing the active loop, frozen prospectives, or one-economic-look rule.

## Hypothesis, not conclusion

A deterioration or surprise in French fiscal/political conditions may create delayed, asymmetric, or cross-asset repricing in some France-vs-Europe expressions. The seed does **not** assume that France must fall, that a crisis must occur, or that any short-France trade has edge.

The research question is narrower:

> When genuinely new France-specific fiscal/political information arrives, is there a public, timestampable, tradable expression whose repricing is systematically delayed, excessive, or incomplete relative to a clean European benchmark?

## L0 fields

- `MECHANISM`: France-specific fiscal/political shocks may propagate unevenly across sovereign rates, banks, equities, credit, volatility and FX; relative markets may incorporate the same information at different speeds.
- `WHO_PAYS`: investors forced to rebalance or hedge after sovereign/fiscal surprise; slower cross-market allocators; mandate-constrained holders; liquidity demanders during stress.
- `WHY_PERSIST`: fragmented information channels, different trading hours/liquidity, mandate constraints, benchmarked portfolios, balance-sheet/friction differences, and low-capacity event windows.
- `CHEAP_DATA_NOW`: public primary sources first (French government/budget publications, debt agency/adjudication releases, Banque de France/INSEE where relevant, EU/Eurostat, rating-agency public announcements when freely timestamped) plus free/public market proxies only where legally and technically usable.
- `CHEAP_EXPRESSION`: relative rather than outright first; candidate surfaces include OAT-vs-Bund sovereign spread, French-bank-vs-European-bank relative moves, CAC-vs-EuroStoxx relative moves, France-sensitive credit/volatility proxies, curve-shape changes, and only secondarily EUR expressions when the shock is demonstrably France-specific.
- `FATAL_PROBLEM`: information already fully priced before the observable timestamp; no reproducible public timestamp; expression dominated by Europe-wide/global factors; insufficient free historical data; effect disappears under conservative costs; outcome-dependent event selection.

## Candidate trigger classes

Use only if timestampable from a public source and definable before market outcome access:

- budget / deficit / debt-path surprises;
- government confidence / coalition / resignation / dissolution / budget-passage events with economically material fiscal implications;
- sovereign-rating or outlook announcements;
- debt-auction demand/coverage anomalies relative to a predeclared baseline;
- France-vs-euro-area macro surprises where the France-specific component can be isolated.

Do not treat generic political headlines as signals without an explicit economic transmission mechanism.

## Candidate expression families

These are discovery surfaces, not approved tests:

1. OAT-Bund spread transmission / overshoot / delayed normalization.
2. French banks vs diversified European banks after sovereign-risk shocks.
3. CAC 40 vs EuroStoxx 50 or matched European sector basket.
4. France-sensitive credit or volatility relative-value, only if public data route is adequate.
5. Cross-market lead/lag: sovereign rates -> banks/equities/credit, or the reverse.
6. Auction micro-events: issuance demand surprise -> secondary-market repricing.

## Anti-confirmation-bias requirements

The L0 researcher must actively seek disconfirmation:

- conditions under which France-specific news is already fully incorporated;
- symmetric positive-surprise cases, not only negative headlines;
- European/global control shocks;
- pre-event drift and leak risk;
- event-time ambiguity;
- alternative explanation from ECB/rates/risk-off moves.

No candidate may be promoted merely because the macro narrative sounds plausible.
