## CANDIDATE_1

MECHANISM = Public disclosure under Section 16 of discretionary insider open-market/private purchases can reveal firm-specific information not yet fully incorporated into price. Purchases are cleaner than sales because they have fewer liquidity/compensation motives; the Form 4 disclosure lag and sparse attention to small/illiquid issuers can leave a short post-disclosure underreaction. Peer-reviewed evidence has repeatedly found positive abnormal returns after insider purchases, and recent filing-date work still reports short-horizon positive abnormal returns while finding much weaker long-horizon drift. Sources: SEC Form 4 instructions https://www.sec.gov/about/forms/form4.pdf ; Bhabra & Hossain (2015) https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rfe.2014.10.001 ; Biggerstaff, Cicero & Wintoki (2020) https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcorpfin.2020.101654 ; SEC 2026 rulemaking discussion citing Jeng et al. and Cohen et al. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/03/2026-04202/holding-foreign-insiders-accountable-act-disclosure
EVENT = Original SEC Form 4 containing a non-derivative transaction code P by an officer and/or director. Do not treat Form 4/A as a new signal. Aggregate multiple qualifying rows for the same issuer/acceptance day into one public event without optimizing purchase-size thresholds or insider subtypes.
WHY_EDGE_CAN_EXIST = Insiders possess superior firm-specific information; a cash purchase is costly conviction; Form 4 can be filed up to the end of the second business day after execution; filings are heterogeneous and often arrive after hours. The economically testable edge is not the insider's transaction-date return but any return remaining after the filing becomes public.
WHY_NOT_ARBITRAGED = Events are sparse, idiosyncratic and capacity-limited; many occur in smaller/less liquid names; parsing multi-owner/multi-row filings and maintaining historical symbol/corporate-action identity is non-trivial; immediate filing reactions reduce available alpha, so only a short residual may remain. This makes the mechanism plausible without assuming it survives costs.
POINT_IN_TIME_DATA = SEC EDGAR original accession documents and headers. Form 4 XML supplies transaction code, transaction date, ownership nature, officer/director flags and contemporaneous issuerTradingSymbol; EDGAR headers expose ACCEPTANCE-DATETIME. Build the event universe from EDGAR filings, not today's security list. Freeze the exact original accession as observed; a later amendment may be audited but must never retroactively replace the signal or be used as an ex-post filter. SEC access documentation: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces and https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data
PRICE_DATA = Use a point-in-time-capable US equity source with daily unadjusted/split-adjusted OHLCV plus ticker/corporate-action history and explicit active+delisted coverage. Suitable accessible options include EODHD delisted/EOD/splits data (https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/delisted-stock-companies-data-2) or Sharadar Prices (https://sharadar.com/prices); Massive/Polygon is also point-in-time and exposes ticker events (https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-does-polygon-handle-ticker-changes-and-acquisitions). Do not use a current-only ticker universe.
DELISTING_HANDLING = Never drop an event because the issuer is later delisted. Resolve the contemporaneous filing symbol to the vendor's point-in-time security identity; retain bankrupt/acquired/delisted names. If trading stops before a test exit, use the last executable price plus explicit merger/cash consideration when the corporate-action feed makes it determinable; otherwise retain the event as unresolved, report it separately, and run a worst-case sensitivity rather than silently deleting it.
TIMESTAMP_INTEGRITY = Signal time is EDGAR ACCEPTANCE-DATETIME, not transaction date, filing-date midnight or a vendor ingestion time. To make execution conservatively observable with daily data, every event enters at the next regular-session open strictly after SEC acceptance, even when the filing was accepted intraday. Filings accepted after market close therefore enter the following regular session. No filing-day close entry is allowed. SEC examples expose accepted timestamps directly on filing index pages; Form 4 itself is due within two business days.
MINIMUM_DISCRIMINATING_TEST = Discovery slice only: 2022-01-01 through 2024-12-31; keep 2025-2026 sealed. Pull all original Form 4 filings, select officer/director non-derivative code-P rows, issuer-day aggregate, and enter at the next regular-session open after acceptance. Measure open-to-close market-adjusted return at +1 and +5 trading sessions versus SPY over the identical executable window. No market-cap, future-volume, future-survival or ex-post amendment filters. Report gross plus one fixed conservative 50 bp round-trip cost stress; cluster inference by issuer and calendar month. PROMOTE_TO_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN only if +5 mean net abnormal return is >0 with a 95% clustered/bootstrap interval above 0, the median is >0, and the sign survives explicit delisted/unresolved sensitivity. KILL if +1 and +5 net effects are non-positive or the apparent effect is created by timestamp/survivorship repair. KEEP_AS_RESERVE otherwise. This test intentionally does not optimize holding period, purchase size, insider rank, liquidity threshold or clusters.
EXPECTED_INFORMATION_GAIN = HIGH. The event is structured, the public timestamp is strong, literature gives a non-zero prior, and a small fixed design can rapidly distinguish "filing reaction already gone by next open" from "residual short-horizon underreaction" while preserving a later confirmation window.
ENGINEERING_COST = LOW_TO_MEDIUM. SEC ingestion and XML parsing are straightforward; the main work is security-identity/corporate-action reconciliation and delisted-price completeness. No NLP model or earnings-consensus history is required.
MAIN_BIAS_RISK = Entering before public acceptance; using transaction date as if public; silently replacing originals with 4/A; dropping dead tickers; ticker reuse; current-universe filtering; adjusted-price misuse around splits; future-volume/liquidity filters; counting multiple rows/owners as independent events.
MAIN_FALSIFICATION = The next regular-session open fully incorporates the informational content, leaving next-open-to-+5 abnormal returns at or below zero after realistic costs and delisting-safe reconstruction.
DATA_BLOCKER = No SEC blocker. A survivorship-safe price/corporate-action source is required; free current-ticker feeds are insufficient. Before running, verify chosen vendor coverage for a small sample of later-delisted Form 4 issuers and ticker changes. If delisted coverage cannot be established, do not promote the experiment.

## CANDIDATE_2

MECHANISM = An initial Schedule 13D reveals a >5% beneficial owner holding with control/influence intent, creating a discrete public update about activist intervention, governance/payout/restructuring pressure and takeover optionality. Historical research and the SEC's own rulemaking analysis document large abnormal returns around these disclosures and some historical post-disclosure drift, but the shorter five-business-day filing regime and strong immediate reaction plausibly compress any residual tradable edge. Sources: SEC final rule https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2023/10/33-11180 ; SEC interpretation https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/exchange-act-sections-13d-13g-regulation-13d-g-beneficial-ownership-reporting ; Becht et al. (2017) https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhx048 ; SEC Federal Register analysis https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-11-07/html/2023-22678.htm
EVENT = Initial Schedule 13D only, not 13D/A, after the revised five-business-day deadline became effective on 2024-02-05. Signal is the original EDGAR acceptance of the filing. Do not use subsequent campaign outcomes, later amendments, press coverage, takeover completion or activist success to select events.
WHY_EDGE_CAN_EXIST = The filing reveals both a large ownership stake and intent to influence/control; successful activism can change payout, governance, capital structure, operations or sale probability. Because the eventual intervention path is uncertain and heterogeneous, investors may update only partially at first disclosure.
WHY_NOT_ARBITRAGED = The headline reaction is well known and often immediate; activists and arbitrageurs watch EDGAR; the 2024 deadline acceleration reduces the old information gap. Residual returns depend on heterogeneous campaign credibility and outcome uncertainty, which creates research friction but also makes a simple unconditional edge less likely. A recent public 2025-2026 event study reported no significant +20-day post-filing drift, so the prior is deliberately below Form 4 rather than assumed positive: https://github.com/fortifiedai/edgar-13d-event-study
POINT_IN_TIME_DATA = SEC EDGAR original Schedule 13D accession, complete submission/header and Item 4 as filed. EDGAR provides original filing date/acceptance metadata; from 2024-12-18 Schedules 13D/13G are required in structured machine-readable form, while earlier 2024 filings require legacy HTML/text parsing. The subject-company CIK in the filing header is the stable issuer key; do not rely on today's ticker map or later amendments. SEC structured-format change: https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/edgar-news-announcements/edgar-release-24-4
PRICE_DATA = Same survivorship-safe source requirement as Candidate 1: daily executable OHLCV plus point-in-time ticker identity, delistings and corporate actions from EODHD, Sharadar or Massive/Polygon. Map the subject-company CIK to the security identity valid on the filing date; where no deterministic mapping exists, mark unresolved rather than fuzzy-matching with future knowledge.
DELISTING_HANDLING = Keep later-delisted activist targets in the event universe. For an exit horizon crossing a delisting/acquisition, incorporate explicit cash/stock consideration when available from point-in-time corporate actions; otherwise retain and stress unresolved cases. No "must still trade at sample end" rule.
TIMESTAMP_INTEGRITY = Use EDGAR acceptance datetime as public signal time. Enter only at the next regular-session open strictly after acceptance; never use trigger/acquisition date, filing-date open, filing-date close before acceptance, a later press article or amendment. The current rule requires initial Schedule 13D within five business days after the trade date that creates the reporting obligation; that regulatory lag is not public alpha before filing.
MINIMUM_DISCRIMINATING_TEST = Discovery slice: 2024-02-05 through 2025-12-31, preserving all 2026 outcomes. Collect every initial Schedule 13D under the new deadline regime, no outcome-based or activist-name cherry-picking. Enter next regular-session open after acceptance; measure SPY-adjusted open-to-close returns at +5 and +20 sessions, with the same fixed 50 bp round-trip stress and issuer/calendar-month clustered inference. PROMOTE_TO_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN only if post-open +5 or +20 net abnormal return has a 95% interval above 0 and remains positive after delisted/corporate-action reconciliation. KILL if both horizons are non-positive or the positive return is confined to pre-filing/acceptance-to-next-open movement that is not captured by the entry rule. KEEP_AS_RESERVE if direction is positive but imprecise or data mapping loss is material. Do not inspect later activist outcomes to rescue the signal.
EXPECTED_INFORMATION_GAIN = MEDIUM. The test is cheap enough and directly answers whether historical 13D drift survived the 2024 deadline change, but prior evidence suggests most repricing may now occur before or immediately around disclosure.
ENGINEERING_COST = MEDIUM. EDGAR collection is easy; deterministic subject-CIK-to-security mapping, mixed legacy/structured formats, Item 4 normalization if later needed, and corporate-action handling are more work than Form 4.
MAIN_BIAS_RISK = Using trigger dates that were not public; contaminating the sample with 13D/A; selecting only famous/successful activists ex post; dropping acquisition/delisting targets; mapping CIK to today's ticker; using future campaign outcomes or press coverage; mixing the old 10-day and new 5-business-day regimes without separation.
MAIN_FALSIFICATION = Under the post-2024 disclosure regime, the tradable next-open-to-+5/+20 return is zero or negative because the market captures the 13D information at filing/overnight and the historical post-disclosure drift has decayed.
DATA_BLOCKER = Deterministic historical subject-CIK-to-traded-security mapping is the main blocker for a clean automated sample, especially for delisted or renamed issuers. Structured 13D improves parsing only from 2024-12-18 onward; earlier 2024 observations need legacy parsing. If unresolved mapping is non-trivial, rank remains below Form 4.
