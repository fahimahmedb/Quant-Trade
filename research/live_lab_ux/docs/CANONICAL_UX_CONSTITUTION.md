1. **Vision.** A credible, desirable spatial world first, discreet technical overlays second. A premium scientific operations diorama with cinematic depth, management-game legibility and architecture that explains the Research OS.
2. **Topology.** One monumental hybrid operations floor, operational ring around a central core, levels, depth, integrated specialized zones, distinct subspaces and converging sightlines. Variable density. Recognizable Research/Mechanisms; Events/Documents; Fundamental; Data/Evidence; Validation/Red Team; Flows/Regulatory; Edge Archaeology/Decay; Implementability; Experiment Infrastructure; Economic Identity; Portfolio/Integration.
3. **Architecture.** Strong architectural lines, real depth, different levels, restrained organic forms, integrated scientific pods, bridges, niches and varied heights. No plain rectangular box or arbitrary collection of WebGL primitives.
4. **Camera.** Low, deep cinematic opening, controlled orbit, zoom, moderate pan, canonical reset. iPhone: natural drag and pinch, generous touch targets. Tap worker → smooth camera focus → short identity → optional details. Information must follow camera focus.
5. **Art direction.** Deep night blue and localized physical amber. Soft night: dark yet readable materials, controlled contrast, no crushed blacks. Architectural night photography; no cyberpunk, violet neon, Matrix, crypto or hacker cockpit.
6. **Materials.** A coherent architectural mix: satin dark metal, brushed steel, smoked glass, dark mineral, technical textile, occasional dark wood, integrated light surfaces. Specialized zones may emphasize different materials.
7. **Detail.** Variable density. Rich areas: documents, books, terminals, instruments, racks, analysis surfaces, boards, organized cables and restrained personal objects. Calm areas reveal floor, circulation and core. Neither empty nor uniformly noisy.
8. **Core.** Iconic vertical scientific column: architectural plinth, internal element, containment, glass/metal, controlled light, optional secondary rings. At idle: elegant, dim, no computation animation. Only explicit reducer-backed Economic Look authorizes progressive brightness/internal activity. Return to rest on completion. Beauty is never evidence.
9. **Workers.** Adult humanoid research agents: adult proportions, elegant silhouettes, simplified faces, credible clothes, premium stylization and subtle human/AI cues. No toys, Lego, child proportions, generic robots or cube people.
10. **Stations.** Discreet futuristic desks, credible furniture, thin screens, instruments, seating, storage and specialist accessories. Screens may show neutral system availability or explicitly decorative architecture. Never invented data, progress, results or economic conclusions.
11. **Gestures.** Readable and activity-specific: reading, coding/testing, evidence manipulation, comparison/validation, economic equipment. Real semantic event → authorized motion. No timer-created work.
12. **Travel.** Important factual transitions may support travel to a zone and cinematic accompaniment. Do not infer destinations from unsupported event semantics.
13. **Idle.** Only light physical ambience: ventilation, mechanical details, reflections, physical light and discreet atmosphere. Workers do not work; core does not compute; no progress advances.
14. **HUD.** Immersive floating corners, maximum three factual signals at rest. Center belongs to the world. No huge title, permanent paragraph, dashboard wall or dominant card.
15. **Mobile.** iPhone is a primary surface. Nearly full-screen immersive 3D. Tap → camera focus → brief identity → second detail level. Bottom sheets only when relevant.
16. **Hierarchy.** Balanced architecture + core + zones + workers. Real activity may temporarily become the focal point; core must not consume the scene.
17. **Rejection.** No empty black room, primitive collection, banking/crypto dashboard, SaaS page with small 3D, childish game, developer prototype, six floating labels on a grid, uniformly black surfaces, or technically correct but visually generic result.
18. **Acceptance.** “I want to explore this place.” Then, with real work: “I can see the laboratory come alive.” Engineering tests are necessary but do not constitute visual acceptance.
