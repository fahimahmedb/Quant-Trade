## Actual defects and descent

Three grouped repair items:

1. The entry guard required both PAUSED and a truthy explanation. pause("") and
   a durable optional null explanation could therefore execute queued work.
   Control state was incorrectly inferred from explanatory text.
2. Dispatch/idle and worker success/failure assigned RUNNING or IDLE without
   preserving an intervening acknowledged pause. A failing worker could recurse
   into a second task, crossing a control boundary after pause returned.
3. The first phase-preservation repair passed 34 new cases. Further falsification
   overlapped pause publication with worker settlement and found two failures:
   publication failure / lost ACK made CampaignState adopt the durable snapshot,
   erasing concurrently updated cycles, budget and processed fingerprint while
   the queue already recorded COMPLETED. Keeping PAUSED alone was insufficient.

These are internal ResearchCampaign interleavings. No future runtime ownership,
cross-module transaction or capability family is implemented.

## Narrow repair

PAUSED is authoritative independently of pause_reason. Activity transitions
preserve PAUSED/STOPPED and recheck before reserving dispatch. A reentrant local
control lock serializes operator pause/resume with durable dispatch and attempt
settlement, including failure rollback from the existing state writer. Failed
worker recursion occurs outside this section. Workers execute outside the lock:
pause remains available while a dispatched worker is running. Completion can
return COMPLETED while the campaign remains PAUSED; that reports the completed
in-flight attempt, not permission to dispatch another one.

The established wire fields, worker interface and explicit resume behavior stay
compatible. A worker already reserved before pause may finish; pause is not a
cancellation API. Explicit resume does not increase the configured cycle budget.
